The data

An imbalanced corpus, and why it matters

Most sentences in the corpus are neutral. A model that always answered “neutral” would look decent on accuracy while being useless, so FinSight is judged by macro-F1, which weights all three classes equally.

The pipeline

How a sentence becomes a score

Normalise

Lower-case and tidy whitespace. Numbers, % and currency symbols are kept because they carry signal.

Vectorise

TF-IDF over single words and two-word phrases: tf × log(N/df).

Score

A linear model computes one score per class: wc·x + bc. The highest wins.

Explain

Each word’s contribution is its weight times the model coefficient. A margin band shows how reliable such predictions were in validation.
